# Faction environment contact sheet

**TabletopHelper · Faction Visual Identity · PR #29**

Review each panorama on the same page. The 20 AI-assisted atmospheric WebP paintings and 16 hand-authored SVG environments are presented in separate groups because their rendering styles differ. These are **original generic sci-fi environment backgrounds**, not official Games Workshop illustrations, faction logos or accurate images of named units.

The layout deliberately keeps text-safe negative space on the left. Judge themes primarily by the environment occupying the **right side**, rather than by the colour alone.

For a larger comparison with **grayscale toggle, hide-names mode and search**, open `/faction-art/gallery.html` on the Vercel preview (login may be required).

## Review checklist

- Does the architecture alone suggest the theme, even in grayscale?
- Are layouts genuinely different, or only recoloured?
- Is there enough low-contrast space for UI text?
- Does a phone crop retain the focal architecture?
- Are the 16 vector studies sufficiently polished to ship, or should specific ones be replaced by full paintings before merging?

See [asset provenance](ASSETS.md) and [implementation notes](README.md). **Do not merge this PR before user approval and an actual device playtest.**
